# Product photo sources

The 12 photographs are local JPEG files linked by `public/data/product-photos.json`. They represent the course API's product categories; the demo names do not identify the photographed brands or exact models. Photo credit and license links are visible below each image in the catalog and product page.

Wikimedia Commons supplied display-size thumbnails for IDs 3–7 and 9–11. Openverse identified the Creative Commons licenses for IDs 1, 2, 8 and 12, whose JPEGs were downloaded from the linked Flickr originals. No further image edits were made.
